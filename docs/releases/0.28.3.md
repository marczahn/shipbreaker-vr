Release 0.28.3 of the Hardspace: Shipbreaker VR mod (PC VR via OpenXR, tested with Quest 3 over Virtual Desktop): a
second way to fly, using things with your hands, and more natural hands and body. Alpha: expect rough edges.

## New
- **Shooter flying** (VR options, Gameplay, *Controller flying*: Off, VTOL or Shooter): the left stick thrusts forward,
  back, left and right, as strongly as you push it; forward is where the left controller points, so tilt it up or down
  to thrust up or down. Hold the left trigger and push the stick left or right to roll. The flight globe shows which
  way and how strongly you thrust and roll. Turning, braking and straightening up work as with VTOL.
- **Use things with your hands**: door switches, consoles, air conditioners and things to collect show their prompt
  only while one of your hands holds them. Grab a switch first, then use it; looking at it is no longer enough.
- **Hands on walls follow you**: holding on flat to a wall or a hull and rolling, the hand turns along on the spot once
  your arm is wound round; turning your body away from the wall, the hand slides along to stay under you.

## Changed
- VTOL thrust is measured where your hand really turns when you tilt it to roll (measured from controller data), so
  rolling no longer shows thrust in the flight globe, and real thrust comes at once also while the hand is tilted.
- Your body turns back in front of your eyes within a few seconds, stays within 40° of where you look, and no longer
  turns after a hand that holds on. After holding on and rolling, one arm no longer looks long with its elbow out.
- Held crates and barrels: a heavy part pushed while you hold on elsewhere stops at your body instead of going through
  it; a part let go only bumps into you again once it is clear of you; held parts no longer tear off in a quick swing;
  the suit calms a held part's spin by its weight; climbing along big loose wreckage pulls you close again.

## Install
Download `ShipbreakerVR-0.28.3.zip`, extract everything into the folder that contains `Shipbreaker.exe` and start the
game. See [docs/INSTALL.md](../INSTALL.md) for details and uninstalling. Your settings from 0.28.2 stay.
