Seventeenth test build of the Hardspace: Shipbreaker VR mod (PC VR via OpenXR, tested with Quest 3 over Virtual Desktop).
Alpha: expect rough edges.

## New
- Upright body (VR options, "Upright body", on by default): your suit's body stays upright and faces your hands while
  you look around, and leans with you. Before, the shoulders swung around your eyes with every look and stayed behind
  when you leaned, so your hands could not reach the controllers when you looked down or to the side. In our test with
  the arms stretched out, the hands now fall short of the controllers by 4 to 11 cm instead of 20 to 24 cm.
- An arm stretched beyond its reach first pushes the shoulder forward a little.

## Known issues
- Holding something heavy with the game's own grab still stops thrust, as in the flat game.
- In the cutter's Stinger mode A does nothing.
- Holding A in the grapple's tether mode while looking at a console uses the console and then recalls your tethers.
- The spots on the body for the tools are a first guess and may need getting used to.
- During load screens the view can still go dark for a blink.
- There is no button for the gamepad's View button, and the left menu button stays with Virtual Desktop.
- Render resolutions above 1.0x can run out of video memory right after loading into a shift, which crashes the game.
  If that happens, lower "Render resolution" to 1.0.

## Install
Download `ShipbreakerVR-0.22.0.zip`, extract everything into the folder that contains `Shipbreaker.exe` and start the
game. See [docs/INSTALL.md](../INSTALL.md) for details and uninstalling. If you update from an earlier version, your
settings stay.
