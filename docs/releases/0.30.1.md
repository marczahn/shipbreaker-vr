Release 0.30.1 of the Hardspace: Shipbreaker VR mod (PC VR via OpenXR, tested with Quest 3 over Virtual Desktop):
**Your momentum is now your own problem.** Alpha: expect rough edges.

## New
- **Second hand on a tool**: take hold of the cutter or the grapple with your free hand wherever you like; its fingers
  wrap round the tool's shape. A part on the grapple weighs on it: the heavier the part, the slower the grapple follows
  your hand.
- **Gliding along hulls**: take hold faster than 2 m/s along a surface and the glove glides on, braking evenly with its
  own force (**Glove brake**, 215 N a hand, two hands brake twice as hard): from 1 m/s within about 23 cm, from 3 m/s
  within about 2 m. Seams take some speed, a step up catches the hand or slows you, at a drop-off the hand lets go, and
  coming in at a slant onto an edge or a bar the hand glides along it. Up to 2 m/s the hand holds at once.
- **Your body has inertia** with **Climbing** set to *Realistic*: your arms speed you up only with their force
  (**Arm force**, 500 N a hand; pushing off over an arm's length gives about 2.2 m/s with one hand, 3.2 m/s with
  both). Taking hold, the hand stops at once and you decide with it: hold it still and the body glides on a little
  before it is stopped (**Catch force**, 900 N) and drawn back; move it back along with your body, hand over hand, and
  you keep your speed and can push on. Too fast to stop within half a meter, the hand is torn off and you fly on.
  *Simple* climbing stays one to one.
- With *Realistic* climbing, twisting your arms on two holds on a hull or the station turns you too, past the
  **Climbing dead zone**.

## Changed
- The hand lies on large things as on a wall, its fingers laid on and curling over edges: the hoist truck, consoles,
  door switches and large parts. Only small things (nuts, bolts, debris) and a lever's grip are held in a fist.
- At an air conditioner's button, broken or working, the fingers stay straight and press it in with their tips.
- The **Climbing dead zone** is 3 cm by default (was 4 cm) for new settings.
- For now your own arm and hand pass through the tool in your other hand; the tool still meets the world.

## Fixed
- The exit door that ends your shift on the MasterJack could not be used. Like the kiosk, it is used with A or X from
  where you look again, with its prompt.
- Some sliders in the VR options were set to their lowest value as soon as the menu opened. They keep your value now.

## Known issues
- At some spots the fingers of an open hand still slip a little into a surface.

## Install
Download `ShipbreakerVR-0.30.1.zip`, extract everything into the folder that contains `Shipbreaker.exe` and start the
game. See [docs/INSTALL.md](../INSTALL.md) for details and uninstalling. Your settings from 0.30.0 stay.
