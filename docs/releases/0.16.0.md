Eleventh test build of the Hardspace: Shipbreaker VR mod (PC VR via OpenXR, tested with Quest 3 over Virtual Desktop).
Alpha: expect rough edges.

## New
- Two new VR tab rows for controller flying: "Thrust deadzone (cm)" and "Full thrust at (cm)". Full thrust always stays
  at least 5 cm beyond the deadzone; changing one row pushes the other along. The flight globe follows the settings.
- The pause menu also opens in the station between shifts, from the controllers.

## Changed
- Full thrust now comes at 10 cm instead of 15 cm; the deadzone stays at 2 cm.
- Pressing the right stick opens the pause menu (in a shift, in the station and on loading screens, where it shows the
  next tip). It used to be X and A together. In menus the press is still the gamepad's right stick press.
- Pressing the left stick puts the view back in front of you. It used to be B and Y together.
- With no button combinations left, A, B, X and Y always act on their own, also when the button on the other hand is
  pressed at the same time.
- The body always turns with your head in VR. The VR tab row "Body follows head" is gone; looking around without
  turning the body made no sense in practice.

## Fixed
- Taking the cutter off your shoulder or the grapple off your hip no longer makes the tool fly into your hand from in
  front of your body: it starts in the hand that took it.

## Known issues
- You cannot grab things with the suit's hands yet; the grip only takes tools.
- The cutter's switch between horizontal and vertical cuts has no button; roll your wrist instead. The part at the
  front of the cutter that shows the angle therefore always stands crosswise.
- The spots on the body for the tools are a first guess and may need getting used to.
- During load screens the view can still go dark for a blink.
- There is no button for the gamepad's View button, and the left menu button stays with Virtual Desktop.
- Render resolutions above 1.0x can run out of video memory right after loading into a shift, which crashes the game.
  If that happens, lower "Render resolution" to 1.0.

## Install
Download `ShipbreakerVR-0.16.0.zip`, extract everything into the folder that contains `Shipbreaker.exe` and start the
game. See [docs/INSTALL.md](../INSTALL.md) for details and uninstalling. If you update from an earlier version, your
settings stay; the old `KoerperFolgtKopf` entry in the config file is simply ignored.
