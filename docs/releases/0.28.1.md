Fix release for 0.28.0 "Immersion 1" of the Hardspace: Shipbreaker VR mod (PC VR via OpenXR, tested with Quest 3 over
Virtual Desktop). Alpha: expect rough edges.

## Fixed
- The right arm no longer stretches when you draw the cutter or the grapple. The game's animation moved its elbow and
  wrist out, so for a moment the arm was far too long and folded, and an arm left longer stayed bent when you held
  yours out straight. Both arms now keep their own length.
- The elbows point down and a little out, as a relaxed arm's do, instead of turning inwards while you draw a tool.

## Changed
- The Stinger only vibrates once the cutter is half way to overheating: faint at first, strong just before it
  overheats. The Splitsaw still hums while it cuts.
- The grapple vibrates the more, the closer the part it holds comes to being lost: towards the edge of where the
  grapple aims, near the end of its range, and fully once the part is out of its view.

## Install
Download `ShipbreakerVR-0.28.1.zip`, extract everything into the folder that contains `Shipbreaker.exe` and start the
game. See [docs/INSTALL.md](../INSTALL.md) for details and uninstalling. Your settings from 0.28.0 stay.
