Release 0.32.1 of the Hardspace: Shipbreaker VR mod (PC VR via OpenXR, tested with Quest 3 over Virtual Desktop):
**Video killed the radio star. But not the radio.** Alpha: expect rough edges.

This is the release after 0.31.5. A 0.32.0 was built and tested but never published; everything it had is in here.

## New: things to collect
Some salvage is worth more to you than to LYNX. Take it home and it stays in your station.
- **AI nodes**: the AI nodes on the ghost ships can be taken. Hold one with the grapple for about four seconds until it
  comes loose (it sits too fast for your hands), then take it and put it in your backpack over your left shoulder. Each
  kind you bring home gets its own place in the station. The inventory list counts the nodes you brought home. LYNX
  notices, of course: expect a message in the station's inbox, and the node is fined like one thrown into the
  processor.
- **A radio**: now and then a radio floats inside a ship (on ships of grade 2 and higher). Put it in your backpack and
  it stands on the table by the station's window. Stand close to it and look at it: **A** plays the music of the
  shifts, **A** again skips to the next song, **B** turns it off. While it plays, the station's own background sound is
  turned down, and the music comes from the radio as you turn your head.
- **Yours when the shift is over**: what you put in your backpack is yours once you end the shift and are back in the
  station. If you leave a shift for the main menu instead, it is as if you had not taken it: the node is on the ship
  again and the radio floats in it again.
- **Each save has its own collection**: nodes, radio and LYNX's messages belong to the save you play. A second career
  starts with an empty station.
- **Without the backpack over the shoulder**: with *Stow over the shoulder* switched off in the VR options, hold the
  node or the radio and press **A** (right hand) or **X** (left hand) to collect it. The game's prompt shows while you
  hold it.
- **LYNX writes to you**: after the tutorial shift a notice from LYNX's employee satisfaction department arrives in the
  station's inbox, in English or German as the game is set. From then on your own posters (`BepInEx\ShipbreakerVR-Poster`)
  can hang in the station.

## New: more work for your hands
- **Strip devices for spare parts by hand.** Computer terminals, data banks and the other devices you strip for the
  truck's parts no longer take a button. Hold the device with one hand and either hold on to the ship with the other
  and pull the device hand away, or close the other hand right beside the first (within about 20 cm) and pull the two
  apart. Keep pulling for three seconds, the controller buzzes harder as it goes. Or make a fist (grip held, nothing
  in the hand) and hit the device three times.
- **Pull a demo charge off the wall.** Hold on to the ship with one hand, hold the charge with the other and pull for
  three to four seconds. The charge stays on its sticky side and lifts only at the edge by your fingers until it
  comes off into your hand.
- **The tool's line runs through your fist.** A tool's line held in a hand is straight inside the fist and bends only
  above and below it. It is also easier to catch: a little further from the hand, and while your hand is moving.
- **A tool held by its housing is put away at its place.** Let it go with any part of it at its place on your body.

## Changed
- **VTOL thrust keeps its direction when you turn round.** With the thrust held, turning your body no longer turns the
  thrust: its reference point goes round with you.
- **Demo charges placed by hand (*Manual*) cling with their back only.** Thrown or drifting against a wall with the
  front or the side, or tilted more than 45 degrees, a charge bounces off. With *Automatic* it clings wherever it
  meets a fitting surface, as before.
- **Demo charges and the game's limit.** The game allows 15 charges out at once (you reach that only after buying more
  at the kiosk in the same shift). Reaching for one more, the visor says so for three seconds and fades. *Automatic*
  gives you no further charge into the hand. *Manual* still does: you can hold and throw it, but it goes on nowhere
  and does not cling until there is room for it again; then it clings by itself wherever it has drifted. A charge you
  leave behind is charged for, as always.
- **Reaching for a tool the other hand holds takes nothing.** The detonator or the cutter no longer jumps into the
  second hand that reaches for its place.
- **In the station, `[X]` and the name of the exit stand at the top of your view while you walk,** and the menus stay in
  front of you while you walk.
- **The Stinger's dots and the grapple's ring lie flat on the surface** you point at; the ring is blue.
- **Prompts name a button only where the button does it.** The hints for flashlight and scanner show no key in VR; the
  word comes up while your hand is at that place at the helmet. A charge on a wall and a device you strip show no
  button while your hand works them. With *Hold on and climb* or *Aim with the hand* switched off, where the buttons do the
  work again, the game's prompts are back.
- **Tool line length** (1 to 5 m) is a setting in the VR options now.
- Own posters can be chosen once LYNX's notice has come, after the tutorial shift. In a career past the tutorial that
  is right away.

## Fixed
- The new body is there in every shift. Before, it was missing from the second shift on until the game was restarted.
- The new body wears one backpack, the thruster pack. It had a second one underneath, and in the pause menu the
  thruster pack hung at your head instead of on your back.
- A demo charge laid on a wall by hand goes on also while your other hand holds on to that ship. It floated away
  there.
- Demo charges with *Automatic*: in tight places full of pipes and panels the preview stays on the wall in front of
  you instead of jumping to one behind it.
- A part held with the grapple no longer shifts when you put the cutter away.
- The grapple stops reeling in when you let go of its button.

## Notes
- What you collected is kept in the mod's config file (a section `[Sammlung <name of your save>]` for each save), not
  in your save game: your save stays as the game wrote it. Removing the mod removes the collection.
- Free play and the weekly ship have no station, so nothing collected there is kept.
- The three blows on a device are an experiment and still silent. `GeraeteSchlag = false` under `[Experimente]` in
  the config file switches them off.

## Known issues
- The new body is a prototype and will be reworked: parts of the suit can go through each other with your arms
  hanging down, and in a deep crouch the cloth over the knee pads stretches visibly.

## Install
Download `ShipbreakerVR-0.32.1.zip`, extract everything into the folder that contains `Shipbreaker.exe` and start the
game. See [docs/INSTALL.md](../INSTALL.md) for details and uninstalling. Your settings from 0.31.5 stay.
