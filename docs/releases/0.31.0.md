Release 0.31.0 of the Hardspace: Shipbreaker VR mod (PC VR via OpenXR, tested with Quest 3 over Virtual Desktop).
Alpha: expect rough edges.

## New
- **Tools you can let go** (with **Extended tool physics** on): let go of the cutter, the grapple or the detonator away
  from its place on your body and it stays afloat as a thing of its own, with the shape of its model, on a 5 m line to
  your hip. Take it again by its grip, or take the line with a free hand and pull the tool in, hand over hand. Let go at
  its place, the tool is put away. Without extended tool physics a tool you let go is back at its place, as before.
- **Demo charges by hand**: the new option **Demo charge placing** chooses how a charge goes on. *Manual*: a charge
  comes into your hand as a thing of its own. Bring its back to a surface and let go, and it goes on as you hold it;
  the line on the charge is the cut. Away from a surface it floats, and you take it again, pass it from hand to hand or
  put it back behind your back. *Automatic* (the default) is the game's way: the trigger places a charge where you
  point, with the preview, or throws it.
- **Taking a charge off a wall by hand**: hold on to it and press A or X for the game's ring, then once more while the
  ring is in its safe zone. With *Manual* the charge comes off into that hand.
- **Charges and detonator have places of their own**: the charges are behind your lower back, for either hand; the
  detonator hangs at your left hip, where you can see it, like the grapple at the right. B no longer switches between
  the two.
- **The detonator's lever**: your index and middle finger press it with the trigger, as far as you pull it.
- **Handedness** in the VR options: *Left* mirrors the places of the tools and the backpack on your body.
- **How many charges are left** shows in the visor while you hold a charge by hand, the charges in your hands counted
  in.
- **Fit tools in the hand** now also fits the detonator, and the hold a charge placed by hand comes into your hand
  with: pass it from hand to hand until it lies right.
- The guide has a picture of where the tools are on your suit.

## Changed
- The tools hang on your body, not on your head: looking around no longer moves them. The grapple hangs 7 cm further
  forward, easier to see and to reach. The backpack sits on the suit's chest instead of the head.
- The cutter is drawn from a larger space over the right shoulder, and goes back within a larger one still.
- A tool you take glides into your hand instead of jumping there.
- Handing a tool over: grip it with the other hand right at its grip. Further along the tool the second hand holds it.
- With the second hand on it the cutter is steadied more, for precise cuts. The grapple still gets quicker.
- A hand that holds a demo charge or the detonator is steadied a little.
- With a demo charge or the detonator in the left hand, Y brakes as with an empty hand.
- A fire on your suit, shaken out, dies down over a second instead of vanishing at once.
- The marks in the middle of the view are gone while you aim with your hands.
- The cutter lies still in your hand; its resting animation no longer moves it.
- The cutter and the grapple sit in the hand as fitted in the headset, for new settings.
- What you let go is left alone by your own arm and hand until it is clear of them, instead of being knocked away.

## Fixed
- Letting a part go with a hand that drifted a little no longer sends the part off.
- A light part held in the hand stays at the hand; it could be pushed some centimeters away after a moment.

## Known issues
- The places behind your back need the controllers to be tracked there. If a charge does not come, reach a little
  further out to the side.
- In free mode the visor shows the game's sign for "without end" instead of a number of charges.
- The left-handed places are new and little tested.
- At some spots the fingers of an open hand still slip a little into a surface.

## Install
Download `ShipbreakerVR-0.31.0.zip`, extract everything into the folder that contains `Shipbreaker.exe` and start the
game. See [docs/INSTALL.md](../INSTALL.md) for details and uninstalling. Your settings from 0.30.1 stay.
