Fifteenth test build of the Hardspace: Shipbreaker VR mod (PC VR via OpenXR, tested with Quest 3 over Virtual Desktop).
Alpha: expect rough edges.

## New
- A (or X in the left hand) on the cutter switches between horizontal and vertical cuts, like the right mouse button
  in the flat game. The part at the front of the cutter turns with it, and the line still turns when you roll your
  wrist. With the cutter in that hand A no longer uses things; use the other hand's A or X.
- Eye sharing (VR options, "Share work between eyes", on by default, F10): the second eye reuses the first eye's visibility
  checks and shadows. In the bay this raised the frame rate from about 70 to 77 fps and cut missed frames from about
  30 % to 7 % in our test. Switch it off if one eye ever shows something the other does not.
- A performance display (VR options, "Performance display", off by default): frame rate, the headset's rate, missed frames and where the
  time goes, in the upper left of the view.

## Changed
- Effects that do not suit VR (motion blur, depth of field, lens distortion, chromatic aberration) are off in VR
  (`VrUnpassendeEffekteAus` under `[Bild]` in the config file).

## Fixed
- What the grapple holds now follows the grapple in your hand. It used to follow your gaze, because the game holds it
  relative to the body, which turns with your head in VR.

## Known issues
- Holding something heavy with the game's own grab still stops thrust, as in the flat game.
- In the cutter's Stinger mode A does nothing.
- Holding A in the grapple's tether mode while looking at a console uses the console and then recalls your tethers.
- The spots on the body for the tools are a first guess and may need getting used to.
- During load screens the view can still go dark for a blink.
- There is no button for the gamepad's View button, and the left menu button stays with Virtual Desktop.
- Render resolutions above 1.0x can run out of video memory right after loading into a shift, which crashes the game.
  If that happens, lower "Render resolution" to 1.0.

## Install
Download `ShipbreakerVR-0.20.0.zip`, extract everything into the folder that contains `Shipbreaker.exe` and start the
game. See [docs/INSTALL.md](../INSTALL.md) for details and uninstalling. If you update from an earlier version, your
settings stay.
