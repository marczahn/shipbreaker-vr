Twelfth test build of the Hardspace: Shipbreaker VR mod (PC VR via OpenXR, tested with Quest 3 over Virtual Desktop).
Alpha: expect rough edges.

## New
- Two tools at once, one in each hand: any two of the cutter, the grapple and the demo charges. Each hand's trigger and
  buttons work its own tool, so you can hold a part with the grapple while you cut another. Every tool still exists
  only once: reaching for the tool in your other hand takes it over. The VR tab row "Two tools at once" (on by default)
  switches this off.
- The scanner hides the tool in your other hand while you scan. Keep that hand's grip squeezed and the tool comes back
  when you let go of the scanner.
- The cutter's plain right side, never seen in the flat game, gets the detail of its left side, mirrored.
  `CutterSpiegeln = false` under `[Bild]` in the config file switches this off.
- Shift+F8 saves the model of the tool in your hand (textures, mesh, UV layout) to `BepInEx\ShipbreakerVR-tools`, for
  modding work.

## Changed
- While the grapple holds something heavy you keep flying and braking with the controller. The flat game stops thrust
  and brake then, so you drifted on or could not get going. The rope keeps pulling and snaps if you fly farther away
  than the grapple reaches.

## Known issues
- You cannot grab things with the suit's hands yet; the grip only takes tools.
- Holding something heavy with a hand still stops thrust, as in the flat game.
- The cutter's switch between horizontal and vertical cuts has no button; roll your wrist instead. The part at the
  front of the cutter that shows the angle therefore always stands crosswise.
- The spots on the body for the tools are a first guess and may need getting used to.
- During load screens the view can still go dark for a blink.
- There is no button for the gamepad's View button, and the left menu button stays with Virtual Desktop.
- Render resolutions above 1.0x can run out of video memory right after loading into a shift, which crashes the game.
  If that happens, lower "Render resolution" to 1.0.

## Install
Download `ShipbreakerVR-0.17.0.zip`, extract everything into the folder that contains `Shipbreaker.exe` and start the
game. See [docs/INSTALL.md](../INSTALL.md) for details and uninstalling. If you update from an earlier version, your
settings stay.
