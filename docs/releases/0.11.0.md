Sixth test build of the Hardspace: Shipbreaker VR mod (PC VR via OpenXR, tested with Quest 3 over Virtual Desktop).
Alpha: expect rough edges.

## New
- The VR settings have their own **VR** tab in the options, next to Gameplay, Video and the others. Each setting shows a
  short hint below the list: what it is for and when to change it.
- The VR settings, their values and hints follow the game's language (German or English).
- Controller flying is now VTOL only ("point and twist" is gone for now). Hold the left trigger, then:
  - move the controller away from where it was when you pressed it to fly that way,
  - turn it left/right to turn, tilt it sideways to roll, tilt it forward/back to pitch; the further, the faster.
- Snap turn moved to the right stick (left/right, 30 degrees). Pressing the left stick still brakes.
- Controller turning also works with "Body follows head" off.

## Fixed
- Tilting the controller to turn also added thrust, because the controller swings around the wrist. Thrust is now
  measured at the wrist.
- The last row of the video options (Contrast) could not be reached with keyboard or gamepad when the list scrolled.

## Known issues
- Tools are still aimed with the head, not with the hands.
- Render resolutions above 1.0x can run out of video memory right after loading into a shift, which crashes the game.
  If that happens, lower "Render resolution" to 1.0.

## Install
Download `ShipbreakerVR-0.11.0.zip`, extract everything into the folder that contains `Shipbreaker.exe` and start the
game. See [docs/INSTALL.md](../INSTALL.md) for details and uninstalling.
