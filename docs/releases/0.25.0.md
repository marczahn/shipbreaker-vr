Nineteenth test build of the Hardspace: Shipbreaker VR mod (PC VR via OpenXR, tested with Quest 3 over Virtual Desktop).
Alpha: expect rough edges.

## New
- From the living area, looking at the work area, the stick to the left goes straight to the terminal, with its own
  prompt.

## Changed
- In the station you stand at the same height everywhere, 1.90 m above the floor as you stood when you last centered the
  view. Lying in bed, sitting on it and the monitors stay as they were.
- The frames and prompts in the station sit on what they point at, seen from where you stand, and stay still when you
  move your head.
- A frame whose target is out of view stays at the edge of the view instead of going missing.

## Fixed
- Holding the stick in the station no longer spins you between two spots: each push moves one spot.

## Known issues
- Leaning your real head into a wall still goes through it, and so does flying in with your head first.
- When using a loose thing such as a cable, the hand can sit a little off it.
- The control pictures do not show the flashlight, the grapple's push and the new move in the station yet.
- Holding something heavy with the game's own grab still stops thrust, as in the flat game.
- In the cutter's Stinger mode A does nothing.
- Holding A in the grapple's tether mode while looking at a console uses the console and then recalls your tethers.
- During load screens the view can still go dark for a blink.
- There is no button for the gamepad's View button, and the left menu button stays with Virtual Desktop.
- Render resolutions above 1.0x can run out of video memory right after loading into a shift, which crashes the game.
  If that happens, lower "Render resolution" to 1.0.

## Install
Download `ShipbreakerVR-0.25.0.zip`, extract everything into the folder that contains `Shipbreaker.exe` and start the
game. See [docs/INSTALL.md](../INSTALL.md) for details and uninstalling. If you update from an earlier version, your
settings stay.
