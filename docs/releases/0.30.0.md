Release 0.30.0 of the Hardspace: Shipbreaker VR mod (PC VR via OpenXR, tested with Quest 3 over Virtual Desktop):
**You've inherited a finger condition known as stubbornness.** Alpha: expect rough edges.

## New
- **Hands that touch things as they look**: the suit's hands meet walls, parts and tools with their real shape. Fingers
  bend back when they press on a wall, the hand folds over and lies flat on it, the palm rounds over edges, and fingers
  that touch something with their backs fold in towards the palm. Hands no longer sink into parts where several faces
  meet or into what lies beside their hold.
- **Hands on tools**: fingers close parallel round the cutter's and the grapple's handle, the thumb just above the index
  finger, the palm folding in under the knuckles. The free hand, the other arm and the tool stop each other instead of
  passing through, and a hand slides along the other arm as along a hull.
- **Fire on the suit**: when the suit catches fire, for example when the cutter overheats, it keeps burning until you
  shake it out: about seven hard strokes back and forth with each hand. It hurts where the game mode deals damage.
- **Climbing** option (Simple or Realistic): with both hands on the same big part, *Simple* keeps you level while you
  move along it. *Realistic* turns you round the part when you twist your arms against each other, past a
  **Climbing dead zone** you can set (4 cm by default). A heavy part no longer turns when you twist your arms on it.

## Changed
- **Left stick**: a short press straightens you up while flying, holding it for 1.2 seconds centers the view.
- **The kiosk** at the start of a shift is used with A or X from where you look, as before, and your hand stays in
  front of it.
- A part in your hand speeds up only as fast as your arm could push it, and throws get no more than that.
- The hand on the fuel line switch sits a little higher and closer to you, and the switch's sound follows your hand.
- The demo charges sit in the left hand with the game's own finger pose.
- **Fit tools in the hand** blocks the other hand's grip only near the tool.

## Fixed
- In the station the eye height stays the same at every **World scale**: no more head in the ceiling.
- With a demo charge in the left hand, the right hand can hold on to the wall again.
- A device used by its button is no longer held by the trigger in the open air.

## Known issues
- At some spots the fingers of an open hand still slip a little into a surface.

## Install
Download `ShipbreakerVR-0.30.0.zip`, extract everything into the folder that contains `Shipbreaker.exe` and start the
game. See [docs/INSTALL.md](../INSTALL.md) for details and uninstalling. Your settings from 0.29 stay.
