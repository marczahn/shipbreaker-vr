Release 0.29.2 of the Hardspace: Shipbreaker VR mod (PC VR via OpenXR, tested with Quest 3 over Virtual Desktop):
**Uh, I wouldn't take it down if I were you. It's a load-bearing poster.** Alpha: expect rough edges.

## New
- **Pull things off the wall**: first aid kits, oxygen and fuel tanks, repair kits and posters can be torn off by
  hand. Hold on to the ship with one hand, take the thing with the other and pull away from the wall. The controller
  shakes, the thing tilts, and after a moment (posters come off sooner) it breaks off with sparks and a tearing sound
  and lands in your hand. Then it floats like any loose part, and over your left shoulder it goes into the backpack.
- **Hear through your suit**: what your hands hold on to, you hear through your suit, also where there is no air,
  like the fuel rushing in a pipe.

## Changed
- **Backpack**: a light, steady buzz while something to collect is over your shoulder, a firm bump when it goes in,
  and a sound right as you let go.
- **Door levers** turn smoothly instead of jumping, stay down while the airlock is still working or your hand is on
  them, hum lightly while they move and knock a little harder at the start and the end.
- **The fuel line switch** hums lightly while it moves.
- **Air conditioners** and things on the wall are held with a flat hand instead of a fist. Pressing a working air
  conditioner's button gives a short buzz.

## Fixed
- The orange spare parts for your own ship now go into the backpack.
- The door lever no longer turns back while your hand is still on it or the airlock is not done yet.
- The fuel line switch no longer flips out and back in again after you used it.

## Known issues
- In vacuum you do not hear things breaking off the wall.
- The fuel line switch's sound comes late.

## Install
Download `ShipbreakerVR-0.29.2.zip`, extract everything into the folder that contains `Shipbreaker.exe` and start the
game. See [docs/INSTALL.md](../INSTALL.md) for details and uninstalling. Your settings from 0.29.1 stay.
