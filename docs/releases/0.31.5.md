Release 0.31.5 of the Hardspace: Shipbreaker VR mod (PC VR via OpenXR, tested with Quest 3 over Virtual Desktop):
**I can wire anything directly into anything! I'm the Professor!** Alpha: expect rough edges.

## New
- **A new body (prototype)**: you wear a new suit of your own, with the game's arms and gloves. It is an early
  prototype, not its final form: expect it to look and move rougher than the rest of the game in places. Switch it in
  the VR options, Graphics, **Body**: *Off* gives you the game's own body back.
- **The detonator's lights** tell what your charges do: yellow while at least one charge is out in the world, green
  while all are with you, blinking red while they go off. The same on the detonator in your hand, at your hip and
  afloat.
- **Demo charge placing *Automatic*, rebuilt**: as with *Manual*, a charge comes into your hand as a thing of its own and
  the detonator into the other hand; bring the charge to a surface and let go, and it goes on as you hold it.
  *Automatic* adds the game's preview of where the charge goes on, at the surface you hold it to or else where you
  point the controller, and the trigger puts it on there; keep the grip squeezed and the next charge comes into your
  hand. A red preview means the place is taken. With extended tool physics, a charge let go with a swing is thrown.

## Changed
- Putting something in your backpack: it is enough that the thing you hold touches the backpack's place over your
  shoulder.
- **Fit tools in the hand**: while it is on, your other hand moves the charge in the hand that holds it, with either
  way of placing, instead of taking it over.
- A demo charge lies in your hand a little differently, as fitted in the headset.
- The right thumb now moves as a whole, like the left one.
- With the thrust mode *Realistic*, straightening up (a short press on the left stick in flight) costs fuel like the
  maneuvering thrusters; with an empty tank it does not turn you. *Simple* and *Standard* stay as they were.

## Fixed
- Big tanks (coolant, fuel) come off as one piece and only with the grapple, as heavy as the game has them; by hand
  they stay on.
- The platform at the kiosk: your hands rest on its floor, no longer above it or sunk into it.
- The pause menu no longer shows demo charges in your hand.

## Known issues
- The new body is a prototype and will be reworked: parts of the suit can go through each other with your arms
  hanging down, and in a deep crouch the cloth over the knee pads stretches visibly.

## Install
Download `ShipbreakerVR-0.31.5.zip`, extract everything into the folder that contains `Shipbreaker.exe` and start the
game. See [docs/INSTALL.md](../INSTALL.md) for details and uninstalling. Your settings from 0.31.0 stay.
