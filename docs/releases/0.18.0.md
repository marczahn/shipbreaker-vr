Thirteenth test build of the Hardspace: Shipbreaker VR mod (PC VR via OpenXR, tested with Quest 3 over Virtual Desktop).
Alpha: expect rough edges.

## New
- Hold on and climb: squeeze the grip of a hand without a tool close to anything, and the hand holds on right there.
  Move the hand to pull yourself along it, hand over hand with both hands, or with one hand while the other works a
  tool. Who moves depends on the masses: the station, fixed hulls and anything too heavy for the game's own grab stay
  put, light parts come to your hand instead. Let go while the hand moves to push off; you keep that speed. While the
  left hand holds on, controller flying pauses. The VR tab row "Hold on and climb" (on by default) switches this off.
- The grip also works the game's controls, such as the airlock consoles: reach for one and squeeze the grip, as an
  alternative to looking at it and pressing A.

## Fixed
- Pulling the trigger with an empty hand could bring out the demo charges behind the game's back and leave their red
  placement marker stuck in the view, impossible to use or put away. Without a tool the trigger and B now do nothing;
  A still uses what you look at.

## Known issues
- Holding something heavy with the game's own grab still stops thrust, as in the flat game.
- The cutter's switch between horizontal and vertical cuts has no button; roll your wrist instead. The part at the
  front of the cutter that shows the angle therefore always stands crosswise.
- The spots on the body for the tools are a first guess and may need getting used to.
- During load screens the view can still go dark for a blink.
- There is no button for the gamepad's View button, and the left menu button stays with Virtual Desktop.
- Render resolutions above 1.0x can run out of video memory right after loading into a shift, which crashes the game.
  If that happens, lower "Render resolution" to 1.0.

## Install
Download `ShipbreakerVR-0.18.0.zip`, extract everything into the folder that contains `Shipbreaker.exe` and start the
game. See [docs/INSTALL.md](../INSTALL.md) for details and uninstalling. If you update from an earlier version, your
settings stay.
