Release 0.28.2 of the Hardspace: Shipbreaker VR mod (PC VR via OpenXR, tested with Quest 3 over Virtual Desktop):
physics for the tools and for what you hold, and fixes for hands, switches and the start of a shift. Alpha: expect
rough edges.

## New
- **Physics tab** in the VR options, with two switches, both off by default:
  - **Extended world physics** (the former "Extended physics", your setting stays): holding on to something fixed,
    the other hand moves heavy parts up to 5 t with a limited force; your body counts as 100 kg in pulls and pushes.
  - **Extended tool physics**: the cutter and the grapple bump into things instead of going through them. Swung
    against something they turn aside about the grip; thrust straight at something they stop at its surface with
    your hand, without sinking in. Pressed on within 15° of straight on they hold, further off they slide, the faster
    the more slanted. They also have weight (**Tool weight**, 5 kg by default, 0 for none): moved fast they lag a
    little behind the controller and settle, and your hand stays on them.
- Smooth turning with the right stick follows the game's look sensitivity (Options, Controls).

## Changed
- Held parts that move (crates, barrels, cabinets) no longer collide with your body while you hold them, and for up
  to 3 s after letting go until you are apart. Drawn in, a part stops a few centimeters short of your body without a
  push. Stopped by a wall, it knocks you back only a little instead of throwing you off.
- With extended world physics, parts above what the game lets you grab move with your hands too: push or throw a
  heavy barrel away while holding nothing else and you and the barrel get the same push in opposite directions.
- Held parts that move are pulled more softly and your suit keeps them from spinning, so a crate no longer swings
  and drags you over it.
- The grapple at your hip is drawn only where your hand really reaches for its grip.

## Fixed
- A hand grabbing a surface out of a movement no longer holds on edgewise with its fingers curled: it takes the face
  its palm turns to.
- Door switches and console levers on a ship: the hand holds them where you take hold, as a fist, and stays there
  while you use them, instead of sitting above the switch and flying off when used.
- Every shift starts with a level view, also one loaded straight from the main menu.
- The VR options entry is in the menu as soon as it opens, and the first VR tab shows at once.
- A tool drawn again starts at your hand instead of swinging in from where you put the last one away.

## Install
Download `ShipbreakerVR-0.28.2.zip`, extract everything into the folder that contains `Shipbreaker.exe` and start the
game. See [docs/INSTALL.md](../INSTALL.md) for details and uninstalling. Your settings from 0.28.1 stay.
