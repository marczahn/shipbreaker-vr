Release 0.29.1 of the Hardspace: Shipbreaker VR mod (PC VR via OpenXR, tested with Quest 3 over Virtual Desktop):
**Orphan screws - AAAhhhh they are in my Hair! They are in my Hair!** Alpha: expect rough edges.

## New
- **Pack it over your shoulder**: hold a cable, circuit board, data cassette or other thing to collect, bring it behind
  your left shoulder and let go. It goes into an imaginary backpack, with a little rattle and a short buzz in the
  controller. Can be switched off in the VR options.
- **Catch the small stuff**: your hands now take hold of small floating debris, and your arms gently knock light
  parts aside as you move through them.
- **Better throws**: thrown parts fly the way your hand moved, spin as your hand turned, and tiny parts fly faster.
- **Collectables with your tools**: the cutter now hits things to collect and the grapple can pull them in.
- **Two new picture options** (both off by default): see small debris from farther away, and let lamps cast shadows
  only nearby, which helps the frame rate in the big ships with many lamps.

## Changed
- Your tools always hang on your body: the cutter on your back, the grapple at your hip. The option for it is gone.
- The grapple sits a little differently in your hand, and the cutter's aiming dots only show on what it can cut.
- Your hand takes hold a little closer to your fingers.

## Fixed
- The broken air conditioner's button no longer uses the wall terminal next to it.

## Install
Download `ShipbreakerVR-0.29.1.zip`, extract everything into the folder that contains `Shipbreaker.exe` and start the
game. See [docs/INSTALL.md](../INSTALL.md) for details and uninstalling. Your settings from 0.29.0 stay.
