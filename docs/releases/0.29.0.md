Release 0.29.0 of the Hardspace: Shipbreaker VR mod (PC VR via OpenXR, tested with Quest 3 over Virtual Desktop):
**Switches and Levers, the World of Mechanics**. Alpha: expect rough edges.

## New
- **Switches and levers you move yourself**: the ship's controls are no longer used with a button press and a fixed
  animation. Grab them and move them with your own hand: turn the airlock lever, fold out and pull down the fuel line
  switch, push in the air conditioner's button. They follow your hand, give way or resist where they should, and you
  feel them in the controller as they unlatch, move and snap in.
- **Glide along the hull**: hold on to the hull and give thrust to slide along it; grab it while flying and you glide
  on and come to a gentle stop.

## Changed
- Switches and levers no longer show prompts; things to collect still do.
- Your hand takes hold of what is in front of your palm, and using a control no longer moves your body.

## Install
Download `ShipbreakerVR-0.29.0.zip`, extract everything into the folder that contains `Shipbreaker.exe` and start the
game. See [docs/INSTALL.md](../INSTALL.md) for details and uninstalling. Your settings from 0.28.3 stay.
