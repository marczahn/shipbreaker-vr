Release 0.29.0 of the Hardspace: Shipbreaker VR mod (PC VR via OpenXR, tested with Quest 3 over Virtual Desktop):
**Switches and Levers, the World of Mechanics**. The ship's controls are worked with your own hands now: turn, pull,
fold and push them. Alpha: expect rough edges.

## New
- **Airlock lever by hand**: grab the yellow grip and turn it 90° until it snaps in, with a light hum while turning and
  a short knock at the end. It stays there while the airlock works and turns back on its own afterwards, also with
  your hand still on it; then bring the controller back to your hand to turn it again. Grab anywhere over the turning
  part and the hand closes around the grip.
- **Fuel line switch (nacelles) by hand**, in the three steps of the game's own animation: fold the handle out, pull it
  down (it goes heavily and shakes the more the further your hand runs ahead), fold it over. It only moves while your
  other hand holds on to something fixed, and stays down once used. Take hold right at its grip.
- **Air conditioner button by touch**: push the big black button with the front of your controller. It sinks in and
  springs back with a short knock in the controller; pushed all the way, it starts the air conditioner, whatever your
  other hand does. The button of a broken air conditioner sinks in and knocks as well.
- **Glide along the hull**: holding on to a hull and giving thrust, the hand slides along with you (at most 1.5 m/s);
  grabbing the hull while flying, you glide on and brake to a stop within less than a meter.

## Changed
- Levers and buttons show no prompts any more and are not used with A: your hands work them. Things to collect and
  breakers keep their prompts and A.
- Controls are taken hold of from just above your palm, not from inside your hand.
- Using a control no longer moves your body to where the game's own animation would put it, and turning a lever from
  afar no longer pulls your body after the hand.

## Install
Download `ShipbreakerVR-0.29.0.zip`, extract everything into the folder that contains `Shipbreaker.exe` and start the
game. See [docs/INSTALL.md](../INSTALL.md) for details and uninstalling. Your settings from 0.28.3 stay.
