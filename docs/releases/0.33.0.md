Release 0.33.0 of the Hardspace: Shipbreaker VR mod (PC VR via OpenXR, tested with Quest 3 over Virtual Desktop):
**The chance to begin again in a golden land of opportunity and adventure.** Alpha: expect rough edges.

This is the release after 0.32.1. The campaign can now be played in VR. (Title: Blade Runner, 1982.)

## New: the campaign in VR
- **The contract at a desk.** Starting a new career, the contract and the screens after it (the cloning, the fees, the
  thumb print) are no longer a tiny terminal in the room: you sit at a copy of your station's desk, with its chair
  and the message terminal, and they are on that terminal's screen. The apartment's picture behind it is larger.
  Choosing an entry moves on to the next one by itself, so you mostly click through. For your name a keyboard shows
  under the screen; point at it with the controllers, which show as two dark grey pens. The game's short halt as your
  profile is made now falls into the fade as you leave the desk.
- **Skipping.** **A** skips videos, the onboarding and the text before the desk, and runs the onboarding's list of
  your debts to its end. In the station **Y** ends the line someone is speaking; `[Y] Skip` shows under the
  subtitles. A skipped line also shortens what waits for it, so the station goes on sooner.
- **The tutorial in VR.** Its tips show the controllers and what to do with them: pictograms for VTOL and Shooter, an
  arrow of its own for each thrust direction, the tools' buttons as the controllers work them and where on your body
  each tool is reached for, and a line for straightening up. The tutorial's steps count what you do with the
  controllers: rolling, thrusting, braking with **Y**, the grapple's push and reeling in, the scanner, drawing a tool
  and recalling tethers.
- **The game's locks hold in VR too.** Where the campaign blocks thrust directions, rolling or the brake (in the
  tutorial, at the end of the campaign), the VR controls do not get around it. Tools are drawn as the game allows
  each of them (the grapple can be taken in the tutorial while the tool wheel is still locked), the detonator only
  shows at your hip once you have the demo charges, and the grapple's tether mode only once you have bought tethers.
- **The station after a shift.** With free walking you walk from the entry at once, at standing height. When a call
  waits, walk to the intercom: one **A** answers it, the prompt comes only once the station's line has finished, and
  the green marker over the intercom goes off as soon as the call is answered. The intercom's light is on while
  anyone speaks in the station, also while you are still in bed.
- **When the campaign shuts your suit down, you can no longer turn.** In the strike shift of act 3 the game stops
  your thrusters; in VR you could still turn and roll with the sticks and your hand. Now, while the suit is off,
  neither stick nor the hand's tilt turns or rolls you, and straightening up is off too: only your head moves, until
  the suit comes back on.

## New: knock on your helmet to fix the radio
- Electrical damage, an explosion or a hard knock now and then make the helmet radio hiss and crackle (RADIO
  MALFUNCTION on the HUD). In VR the game no longer knocks your helmet for you: knock twice against the side of your
  helmet with either hand (each knock is heard, the game's own knock sound) and the radio works again at once. Left
  alone, it clears by itself after about 20 seconds, as before. The hiss is a little louder. Until you have knocked
  once, a short line with a little figure under the warning tells you how (set `Hinweise/FunkKlopfenGelernt` in the
  config back to false to see it again).

## New: hands
- **The empty hand closes as far as you squeeze.** Away from any surface, the grip closes an empty hand into a fist
  as far as you press it and opens it the same way, no longer all at once.
- **A hand on a hold turns with your controller.** Holding on to a wall, an edge or a handle, the hand turns as your
  controller does, a little behind it as if it rubbed on the surface, from the way you took hold. The elbow and the
  forearm turn along. Handles are held open on their front, and the thumb curls over an edge as the fingers do.
- **Light things lie fast in the hand.** A part up to 10 kg that you carry (a radio, a box) is one with your hand:
  it turns with your hand, and the hand no longer turns on it. On walls, the hull and heavier parts the hand still
  turns on the surface with your controller.
- **The hand goes along with a tool's recoil**, and the grip stays in the hand.
- **The cutter held with two hands is clearly steadier**, its place as well as its turn (with extended tool physics).

## New: tools on the body
- **Place your tools yourself: VR options → Place tools on the body.** Switched on, the grip at the grapple (right
  hip) or the detonator (left hip) takes the hanging tool itself instead of drawing it; it moves with your hand and
  stays where you let go. The places are kept.
- **The wall comes before the tools.** With a hand at one of your tools' places and a wall or the hull within reach,
  the grip holds on to the wall; to draw the tool, turn a little away from it.
- **The grapple's model has no more holes**: the gaps in its jaws and frame are closed.

## New options
- **Comfort → Immersive computer screens: On / Large screens.** On shows the station's computer screens on their
  monitors in the room, as before. Large screens shows each of them as large as the menus, fixed in the room where
  the monitor is (at the contract's desk too), so every detail stays readable with a weaker headset or glasses.
- **Comfort → Hand smoothing** (0 to 3, off by default). It smooths the hands a little, so their natural tremor shows
  less when they are close to your face or in videos. A hand moved fast is followed at once.
- **Physics → Extended body physics** (off by default). Throwing a part, a tool or a charge pushes you back, and your
  body weighs 100 kg for throwing, the tool's line and pushing parts, instead of the game's 50 kg.

## Changed
- **The HUD's brackets show bars**: your speed on the left (full at 3.5 m/s, the fully upgraded jetpack; red from
  the speed where a crash hurts) and the air pressure on the right.
- **The HUD in a shift and the loading screens are drawn sharper**, as the station's screens are.

## Fixed
- **Button prompts in the menus show the VR controllers' buttons**, also right after a key on the keyboard (like F3)
  was pressed. Before, the main menu and others could show keyboard keys in VR.
- **The grapple reels in only while you hold the button, in VR and on the flat screen.** Grappled to a wall and
  reeling in, the game's rope went on pulling you after you let go, sometimes faster and faster, and the next press
  then often did nothing for a while. This is how the original game behaves (its rope length runs ahead of you when
  you are fully upgraded); it only shows much more in VR. Now the pull stops the moment you let go and starts the
  moment you press, and a press made while the grapple was still flying is no longer lost.
  **This fix is also active in the normal flat-screen game (after switching VR off with F3), for as long as the VR
  mod is installed.**

## Notes
- Hand smoothing used to be an experiment that was on (`Experimente/HandGlaettung`). It is now the comfort option
  and starts off; set it again under Comfort if you liked it.

## Known issues
- The new body is still a prototype: parts of the suit can go through each other with your arms hanging down, and in
  a deep crouch the cloth over the knee pads stretches visibly.
- At the very end of the campaign, holding on and pushing off with your hands still works where the game holds you
  in place.

## Install
Download `ShipbreakerVR-0.33.0.zip`, extract everything into the folder that contains `Shipbreaker.exe` and start the
game. See [docs/INSTALL.md](../INSTALL.md) for details and uninstalling. Your settings from 0.32.1 stay.
