Release 0.33.0 of the Hardspace: Shipbreaker VR mod (PC VR via OpenXR, tested with Quest 3 over Virtual Desktop):
**(working title, Raik chooses it)** Alpha: expect rough edges.

This is the release after 0.32.1. It is still being worked on; this file grows with it.

## New: your own fingers
- **The empty hand follows your fingers on the controller.** The grip closes it into a fist as far as you press it,
  no longer all at once. The index finger is out straight while it is off the trigger and closes as you pull it. The
  thumb is out while it is off the buttons and the stick. So you can point, make a fist and give a thumbs up. It needs
  a controller with touch sensors, as the Quest's; without them the hand closes with the grip.
- **A stiff index finger.** Squeeze the grip away from any surface with your index finger off the trigger: a fist with
  the index finger out, and that finger is stiff. It pushes an air conditioner's button in, and pushed against a wall
  it pushes you off, slowly, because a finger is not an arm. Close to a surface the grip holds on as always.

## Changed
- **The hands are smoothed a little**, so their natural tremor shows less when they are close to your face. A hand
  moved fast is followed at once. `Experimente/HandGlaettung` in the config sets how much (0 for none). This was made
  for videos and may be taken out again.
