Release 0.33.0 of the Hardspace: Shipbreaker VR mod (PC VR via OpenXR, tested with Quest 3 over Virtual Desktop):
**The chance to begin again in a golden land of opportunity and adventure.** Alpha: expect rough edges.

This is the release after 0.32.1. The campaign can now be played in VR. (Title: Blade Runner, 1982.)

## New: a fist that follows your grip
- **The empty hand closes as far as you squeeze.** Away from any surface, the grip closes an empty hand into a fist
  as far as you press it and opens it the same way, no longer all at once.

## New: a new career starts at a desk
- **The contract at a desk.** Starting a new career, the contract and the screens after it (the cloning, the fees, the
  thumb print) are no longer a tiny terminal in the room: you stand at a copy of your station's desk, with its chair
  and the message terminal, and they are on that terminal's screen. The apartment's picture behind it is larger.

## Changed
- **Knock on your helmet to fix the radio.** Electrical damage, an explosion or a hard knock now and then make
  the helmet radio hiss and crackle (RADIO MALFUNCTION on the HUD). In VR the game no longer knocks your helmet for
  you: knock twice against the side of your helmet with either hand (each knock is heard, the game's own knock
  sound) and the radio works again at once. Left alone, it clears by
  itself after about 20 seconds, as before. The hiss is a little louder. Until you have knocked once, a short line
  with a little figure under the warning tells you how (set `Hinweise/FunkKlopfenGelernt` in the config back to
  false to see it again).
- **New comfort option: Comfort → Hand smoothing** (0 to 3, off by default). It smooths the hands a little, so their
  natural tremor shows less when they are close to your face or in videos. A hand moved fast is followed at once.
- **Light things lie fast in the hand.** A part up to 10 kg that you carry (a radio, a box) is one with your hand:
  it turns with your hand, and the hand no longer turns on it. On walls, the hull and heavier parts the hand still
  turns on the surface with your controller.

## Fixed
- **When the campaign shuts your suit down, you can no longer turn.** In the strike shift of act 3 the game stops
  your thrusters; in VR you could still turn and roll with the sticks and your hand. Now, while the suit is off,
  neither stick nor the hand's tilt turns or rolls you, and straightening up is off too: only your head moves, until
  the suit comes back on.
- **Button prompts in the menus show the VR controllers' buttons**, also right after a key on the keyboard (like F3)
  was pressed. Before, the main menu and others could show keyboard keys in VR.
- **The grapple reels in only while you hold the button, in VR and on the flat screen.** Grappled to a wall and
  reeling in, the game's rope went on pulling you after you let go, sometimes faster and faster, and the next press
  then often did nothing for a while. This is how the original game behaves (its rope length runs ahead of you when
  you are fully upgraded); it only shows much more in VR. Now the pull stops the moment you let go and starts the
  moment you press, and a press made while the grapple was still flying is no longer lost.
  **This fix is also active in the normal flat-screen game (after switching VR off with F3), for as long as the VR
  mod is installed.**

## New comfort option: immersive computer screens
- **Comfort → Immersive computer screens: On / Large screens.** On shows the station's computer screens on their
  monitors in the room, as before. Large screens shows each of them as large as the menus, right in front of you where
  the monitor is, so every detail stays readable with a weaker headset or glasses.
