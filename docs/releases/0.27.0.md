Twenty-first test build of the Hardspace: Shipbreaker VR mod (PC VR via OpenXR, tested with Quest 3 over Virtual Desktop).
Alpha: expect rough edges.

## New
- Thrust mode in the VR tab: "Simple" brakes you to a stop by yourself when you let go of thrust, at a third of the
  brake's strength and fuel. "Standard" is the game's own behavior. "Realistic" switches off the game's automatic
  brake, so you keep your speed.
- Hold the left stick in to straighten up to the horizon. A short press still centers the view.
- Push heavy parts (off by default): with one hand braced on something fixed, the other hand moves parts up to 5 tons
  with a limited force of 250 N. They gather speed slowly, and where you hold them decides whether they move or turn.
- The grapple hangs visibly at your right hip while it is not in a hand, with its grip where you draw it from.
- Three dots circle the end of the Stinger's beam when a part is within its reach.
- Hand tilt in the VR tab tips the hands and tools up to 40 degrees against the controllers.
- Visor height and Screen sharpness in the VR tab. The station's screens are drawn sharper while you use them.

## Changed
- The VR tab has three groups: Comfort, Gameplay and Graphics.
- The grapple is 20 percent smaller in the hand.
- The grapple's zone at the hip lies further out and further back.
- The laser pointers go off while the cutter fires and while the grapple holds something.
- The visor sits 2 cm lower by default.
- The tool export for modding is gone from the mod.

## Fixed
- The speakers' portraits show as they should in missions and at the station.
- The yes/no prompts show in VR, for example when quitting from the main menu.
- No colour cast when waking up.

## Known issues
- The headings in the VR tab are rows without a function: you can select them, nothing happens.
- Walking with a stick can make you feel sick. Turning is in steps, but there is no teleport yet.
- While walking the head can lean out of the floor through walls and furniture.
- Two prompts of the walking mode are plain text: `[A]` is not the game's button symbol, and X has no prompt.
- The door beside the bed has no use.
- Leaning your real head into a wall still goes through it, and so does flying in with your head first.
- When using a loose thing such as a cable, the hand can sit a little off it.
- Holding something heavy with the game's own grab still stops thrust, as in the flat game.
- Holding A in the grapple's tether mode while looking at a console uses the console and then recalls your tethers.
- During load screens the view can still go dark for a blink.
- There is no button for the gamepad's View button, and the left menu button stays with Virtual Desktop.
- Render resolutions above 1.0x can run out of video memory right after loading into a shift, which crashes the game.
  If that happens, lower "Render resolution" to 1.0.

## Install
Download `ShipbreakerVR-0.27.0.zip`, extract everything into the folder that contains `Shipbreaker.exe` and start the
game. See [docs/INSTALL.md](../INSTALL.md) for details and uninstalling. If you update from an earlier version, your
settings stay, except "Simple thrust", which is now "Thrust mode" and starts on Standard.
