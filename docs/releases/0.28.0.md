Twenty-second test build of the Hardspace: Shipbreaker VR mod (PC VR via OpenXR, tested with Quest 3 over Virtual Desktop).
Alpha: expect rough edges. This is the first "Immersion" release: hands, touch and physics.

## New
- Your hands look like they hold what they hold. They close around the cutter, the grapple and small parts, make a
  loose fist with the grip squeezed or on a door switch, and lie flat on walls, hulls, consoles and large parts, as if
  held by a magnet in the glove. Fingers curl over edges.
- A hand that holds on stays fixed where it holds: it no longer turns with the controller, so it does not turn into
  the thing held, and a flat hand is kept out of the surface. Only your arm moves around it.
- The controllers vibrate: the hand with the cutter hums while it cuts (stronger with the Splitsaw), the hand with the
  grapple hums while it holds a part (more for heavier ones), and each tether end you fire gives a short buzz.
- Holding two heavy parts, one in each hand, pushes them apart, shared by their masses.
- "VR options" in the main menu and the pause menu, above "Options": the VR settings in three tabs, Comfort, Gameplay
  and Graphics, instead of one long VR tab next to the game's.
- "Turn around" in the VR options: the right stick turns you around your head's up, as before, or around your body's.

## Changed
- "Push heavy parts" is now "Extended physics" in the Gameplay group. Heavy parts gather speed more slowly the heavier
  they are: at most 0.4 m/s² at 200 kg, down to 0.2 m/s² at 1000 kg.
- "Fit tools in the hand" moved to the Comfort group.
- The grapple at the hip sits 1 cm further out and 2 cm further back.
- Near a door switch or console button, grabbing goes onto the switch itself instead of the wall under it.

## Fixed
- The helmet stays hidden in a new shift after you took it off in VR.
- Rowing with both arms while holding a light part such as a barrel built up speed. Partly fixed; see below.
- At the station, closing a screen and pressing X right away could leave you unable to walk or open anything. A and X
  now wait 1.5 seconds after a screen closes.

## Known issues
- Holding a light part such as a 60 kg barrel with both hands and rowing with your arms can still build up some speed.
- Where a hull's visible skin lies outside its collision shape, a flat hand can still sink a few centimeters into it,
  and fingers can curl over edges you cannot see.
- Walking with a stick can make you feel sick. Turning is in steps, but there is no teleport yet.
- While walking the head can lean out of the floor through walls and furniture.
- Two prompts of the walking mode are plain text: `[A]` is not the game's button symbol, and X has no prompt.
- The door beside the bed has no use.
- Leaning your real head into a wall still goes through it, and so does flying in with your head first.
- Holding something heavy with the game's own grab still stops thrust, as in the flat game.
- Holding A in the grapple's tether mode while looking at a console uses the console and then recalls your tethers.
- During load screens the view can still go dark for a blink.
- There is no button for the gamepad's View button, and the left menu button stays with Virtual Desktop.

## Install
Download `ShipbreakerVR-0.28.0.zip`, extract everything into the folder that contains `Shipbreaker.exe` and start the
game. See [docs/INSTALL.md](../INSTALL.md) for details and uninstalling. If you update from an earlier version, your
settings stay; "Extended physics" keeps what "Push heavy parts" was set to.
