Ninth test build of the Hardspace: Shipbreaker VR mod (PC VR via OpenXR, tested with Quest 3 over Virtual Desktop).
Alpha: expect rough edges.

## New
- Flight globe: a small globe at the bottom of the visor shows where the left controller is while you hold the
  trigger. Its center is where you pressed it, its surface full thrust, the inner globe the deadzone; a small cross
  moves and turns with your hand and turns orange while the trigger is held. The row "Flight globe" in the VR tab
  shows it always, only while flying, or never.
- B and Y pressed together center the view, like F4.
- New VR tab rows "Head height" and "Head sideways": move your eyes up to 10 cm up/down or left/right against the
  game's head position, in whole centimeters. The helmet stays put, only the eyes move inside it.

## Changed
- Turning and pitching moved from the left stick to the right stick, so the left hand flies and the right hand steers.
- In menus only the stronger direction of the left stick counts, so adjusting a value with left/right no longer
  jumps to the row above or below.

## Fixed
- A view of the station that flashed up during load screens should be gone (a camera from the previous scene was
  switched back on).

## Known issues
- During load screens the view can still go dark for a blink.
- There is no button for the gamepad's View button, and the left menu button stays with Virtual Desktop.
- Tools are still aimed with the head, not with the hands.
- Render resolutions above 1.0x can run out of video memory right after loading into a shift, which crashes the game.
  If that happens, lower "Render resolution" to 1.0.

## Install
Download `ShipbreakerVR-0.14.0.zip`, extract everything into the folder that contains `Shipbreaker.exe` and start the
game. See [docs/INSTALL.md](../INSTALL.md) for details and uninstalling.
