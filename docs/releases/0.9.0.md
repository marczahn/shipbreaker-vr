Fourth test build of the Hardspace: Shipbreaker VR mod (PC VR via OpenXR, tested with Quest 3 over Virtual Desktop).
Alpha: expect rough edges.

## New
- Fly with the left motion controller. Choose the scheme in the options row "VR: Controller flying":
  - **Point and twist** (default): point the controller and push the stick forward to fly that way, pull it back to
    fly backwards. Hold the trigger and twist or tilt your hand to roll, pitch or turn.
  - **VTOL**: hold the trigger and move the controller away from where it was when you pressed it to fly that way;
    turn it to roll, pitch or turn.
  - In both: stick left/right snap-turns 30 degrees, pressing the stick brakes.
  - The grip button stays free for grabbing in a later version.

## Known issues
- Tools are still aimed with the head, not with the hands.

## Install
Download `ShipbreakerVR-0.9.0.zip`, extract everything into the folder that contains `Shipbreaker.exe` and start the
game. See [docs/INSTALL.md](../INSTALL.md) for details and uninstalling.
