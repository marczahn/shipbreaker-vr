Third test build of the Hardspace: Shipbreaker VR mod (PC VR via OpenXR, tested with Quest 3 over Virtual Desktop).
Alpha: expect rough edges.

## New
- VR starts together with the game (`[Rendering] VrBeimStart`, on by default), so the launch intro, the main menu
  and all loading screens already show in the headset. F3 still switches VR on and off.
- Intro and loading screens appear on the menu canvas in front of you on black, instead of a frozen or half-loaded
  world.
- F4 now centers everything on your current head pose, also in menus: the view direction turns to where you look and
  the menu canvas is placed straight in front of your eyes.

## Fixed
- Grey screen with oversized scanlines on the loading screen before a mission (e.g. free play after confirming the
  mission): the game's load screen camera rendered its own image and CRT effects into the headset on top of
  everything.
- Crash (out of video memory) at the start of a mission when the render resolution is above 1.0: while loading, the
  resolution drops to the headset's native 1.0 and returns to your setting afterwards if it fits into memory.
- The game's full-screen glitch and CRT post effects no longer fill the view with grey in VR.
- Removed the blue debug tint from the UI.

## Known issues
- The scanline look of the loading screen is missing in VR.

## Install
Download `ShipbreakerVR-0.8.0.zip`, extract everything into the folder that contains `Shipbreaker.exe` and start the
game. See [docs/INSTALL.md](../INSTALL.md) for details and uninstalling.
