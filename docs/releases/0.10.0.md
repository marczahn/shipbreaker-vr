Fifth test build of the Hardspace: Shipbreaker VR mod (PC VR via OpenXR, tested with Quest 3 over Virtual Desktop).
Alpha: expect rough edges.

## New
- The station between shifts works in VR: the view follows your head, the prompts show, and moving between spots
  fades to black and back instead of the game's camera moves (the flat game keeps them).
- The screens at the terminal, the workbench and above the bed show their content on the monitor itself, with a
  screen look (scan lines, glass, glow). **F7** at a monitor switches the look, `[Anzeige] MonitorEffekt` in the config.
- Waking up in bed you look up at the monitor with the morning menu.
- The station has its own render resolution: options row "VR: Station resolution" (0.75 by default, at most 1.0),
  independent of "VR: Render resolution". If the view wobbles slightly at the monitors when you move your head, lower
  it until that stops.
- Menus always appear upright at eye height in the direction you face, also with F4 and when you look up or down. When
  you stand up or sit down, they follow your eye height once it is more than 30 cm off.
- The visor HUD no longer copies every small tremor of the head: options row "VR: Visor steadying" (Off, Light by
  default, Strong).
- Options row "VR: Ambient occlusion" (Low by default, Off, Game default). Low makes the station much faster.
- **F8** writes what is drawn around you into the log and saves two images to `BepInEx\ShipbreakerVR-F8`, for bug
  reports.

## Fixed
- In the station the head position was fixed, it stuttered heavily (the bay was rendered as well) and the R/Tab
  prompts were missing.
- After loading a career from the main menu, the load screen looked frozen in VR.
- The view out of the station's window and the earth seen from the terminal were cut off.
- A black rectangle hung in the room after loading or waking up in the station. The game's fades now darken the whole
  view.
- The prompt to answer the phone at the station's terminal was missing.
- Entering the station above 1.0x ran out of video memory.

## Known issues
- Tools are still aimed with the head, not with the hands.
- The station costs the graphics card noticeably more than the bay: an RTX 4070 Ti needed 0.7 to reach 90 Hz at Quest 3
  resolution.

## Install
Download `ShipbreakerVR-0.10.0.zip`, extract everything into the folder that contains `Shipbreaker.exe` and start the
game. See [docs/INSTALL.md](../INSTALL.md) for details and uninstalling.
