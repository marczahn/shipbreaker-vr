Tenth test build of the Hardspace: Shipbreaker VR mod (PC VR via OpenXR, tested with Quest 3 over Virtual Desktop).
Alpha: expect rough edges.

## New
- Aim with your hands: cutter, grapple, scanner, demo charges, grabbing and interacting aim where the controller that
  holds the tool points, from the tool's muzzle. Rolling your wrist rolls the cutter's line, and thrown demo charges fly
  where you point.
- The tools sit in your hand. Take them off your suit by squeezing the grip: grapple at the right hip, demo charges at
  the left hip, cutter over the right shoulder, scanner at the right side of the helmet. Keep the grip squeezed to hold
  the tool, let go to put it away.
- Either hand can take a tool, one at a time; reaching for it with the other hand hands it over. In the left hand X and
  Y work like A and B, and controller flying pauses until you let go.
- Tool buttons on the controller that holds the tool: trigger uses the tool, A uses what you point at, trigger + A
  throws, B switches the tool's mode, trigger + B reels in, holding B places a tether, holding B while pointing at
  nothing recalls the tethers.
- A thin laser shows where you aim; its dot takes the color of the game's crosshair (for example when the grapple has a
  target in range). The crosshair in the middle of the view is hidden while you aim with the hand.
- The suit's hands follow your controllers and close while you squeeze the grip.
- New VR tab row "Aim with the hand": off aims the tools at the center of the view again.
- New VR tab row "Fit tools in the hand": grab the cutter or the grapple with the other hand and move it until it sits
  right in your hand.
- A controls picture for the tools in the install guide.

## Changed

## Known issues
- Demo charges are always held by the suit's right hand, even when you take them with the left; that arm then reaches
  across.
- You cannot grab things with the suit's hands yet; the grip only takes tools.
- The spots on the body for the tools are a first guess and may need getting used to.
- During load screens the view can still go dark for a blink.
- There is no button for the gamepad's View button, and the left menu button stays with Virtual Desktop.
- Render resolutions above 1.0x can run out of video memory right after loading into a shift, which crashes the game.
  If that happens, lower "Render resolution" to 1.0.

## Install
Download `ShipbreakerVR-0.15.0.zip`, extract everything into the folder that contains `Shipbreaker.exe` and start the
game. See [docs/INSTALL.md](../INSTALL.md) for details and uninstalling.
