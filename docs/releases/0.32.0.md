Release 0.32.0 of the Hardspace: Shipbreaker VR mod (PC VR via OpenXR, tested with Quest 3 over Virtual Desktop):
**Sammelkampagne** Alpha: expect rough edges.

## New: things to collect
Some salvage is worth more to you than to LYNX. Take it home and it stays in your station.
- **AI nodes**: the AI nodes on the ghost ships can be taken. Pull one off with your hand (it sits fast: keep pulling
  for five to six seconds), or hold it with the grapple for a few seconds until it comes loose, and put it in your
  backpack over your left shoulder. Each kind you bring home gets its own place in the station. The inventory list
  counts the nodes in your backpack. LYNX notices, of course: expect a message in the station's inbox, and the node is
  fined like one thrown into the processor.
- **A radio**: now and then a radio floats inside a ship (on ships of grade 2 and higher). Put it in your backpack and
  from then on it stands on the table by the station's window. Stand close to it and look at it: **A** plays the music
  of the shifts, **A** again skips to the next song, **B** turns it off. While it plays, the station's own background
  sound is turned down, and the music comes from the radio as you turn your head.
- **LYNX writes to you**: after the tutorial shift a notice from LYNX's employee satisfaction department arrives in the
  station's inbox, in English or German as the game is set. From then on your own posters (`BepInEx\ShipbreakerVR-Poster`)
  can hang in the station.

## Changed
- Own posters can be chosen once LYNX's notice has come, after the tutorial shift. In a career past the tutorial that
  is right away.

## Notes
- What you collected is kept in the mod's config file (section `[Experimente]`), not in your save game: your save stays
  as the game wrote it. Removing the mod removes the collection.

## Known issues
These will be fixed in 0.32.1.
- The radio does not show up in the station after you collect it. It is not lost: it is kept and will stand on the
  table once 0.32.1 is installed.
- The large round AI node may not sit right on the white crate in the station.

## Install
Download `ShipbreakerVR-0.32.0.zip`, extract everything into the folder that contains `Shipbreaker.exe` and start the
game. See [docs/INSTALL.md](../INSTALL.md) for details and uninstalling. Your settings from 0.31.5 stay.
