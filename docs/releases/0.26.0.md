Twentieth test build of the Hardspace: Shipbreaker VR mod (PC VR via OpenXR, tested with Quest 3 over Virtual Desktop).
Alpha: expect rough edges.

## New
- Walk through the station: the option "Walk in the station" in the VR tab replaces the spots. It is off by default, and
  the spots stay as they are. Sitting on the bed, A stands up; the left stick walks at 1.8 m per second, the right stick
  turns in steps of 45 degrees, and you slide along the edge of the floor.
- Near the terminal, the workbench or the exit, A uses them while you stay where you are and can move your head; B leaves
  them as usual. X opens the exit from anywhere. There are no black fades in between.
- Own posters: PNG and JPG files in `BepInEx\ShipbreakerVR-Poster` join the pictures for the posters, at either way of
  using the station. They keep their shape, and your choice survives new pictures from the game.
- While walking, look at a poster from close by and change its picture with the triggers.

## Changed
- While walking, the frames and prompts of the spots and the colour cast at the screens stay away.

## Fixed
- With "Debug/Diagnose" on, B and Y together also make a dump, and the station's floor plan goes into a file of its own.

## Known issues
- Walking with a stick can make you feel sick. Turning is in steps, but there is no teleport yet.
- While walking the head can lean out of the floor through walls and furniture.
- Coming back from a shift has not been tested with walking on; the station may start with the spots until you sleep.
- Two prompts of the walking mode are plain text: `[A]` is not the game's button symbol, and X has no prompt.
- The door beside the bed has no use.
- Leaning your real head into a wall still goes through it, and so does flying in with your head first.
- When using a loose thing such as a cable, the hand can sit a little off it.
- The control pictures do not show the flashlight, the grapple's push and the moves in the station yet.
- Holding something heavy with the game's own grab still stops thrust, as in the flat game.
- In the cutter's Stinger mode A does nothing.
- Holding A in the grapple's tether mode while looking at a console uses the console and then recalls your tethers.
- During load screens the view can still go dark for a blink.
- There is no button for the gamepad's View button, and the left menu button stays with Virtual Desktop.
- Render resolutions above 1.0x can run out of video memory right after loading into a shift, which crashes the game.
  If that happens, lower "Render resolution" to 1.0.

## Install
Download `ShipbreakerVR-0.26.0.zip`, extract everything into the folder that contains `Shipbreaker.exe` and start the
game. See [docs/INSTALL.md](../INSTALL.md) for details and uninstalling. If you update from an earlier version, your
settings stay.
