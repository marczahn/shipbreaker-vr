Eighteenth test build of the Hardspace: Shipbreaker VR mod (PC VR via OpenXR, tested with Quest 3 over Virtual Desktop).
Alpha: expect rough edges.

## New
- Grabbing is much more forgiving: the hand reaches 15 cm, a grip pressed just before the hand arrives still catches, and
  the hand snaps to the surface. Switches that have no solid part can be held too.
- Small parts up to 30 kg sit firmly in the hand and turn with it. Heavier ones lag behind the hand more and more.
- The grapple pushes things away with A when nothing is grappled, also at consoles; hold A to charge. It goes where the
  grapple points. Consoles are used with an empty hand.
- The left hand at the left side of the helmet switches the flashlight with the grip.
- An empty hand stops at walls instead of going through them.
- Your body stays upright and faces your hands while you look around, so the arms reach the controllers the same way
  wherever you look. The row "Upright body" in the VR tab compares it with the old behavior.
- Cutter and grapple are steadier and have a little weight when you swing them.

## Changed
- Pulling yourself to a wall no longer makes a series of impacts: you stop softly just short of it, your face too. The
  game's hand stays on its hold however far your real hand goes, and nothing pushes you away until your hand has come
  back past it. The end of a pull brings you closer than your arm alone could.
- The body's hitbox is smaller and sits lower and further back, so airlocks are no longer a tight fit.
- The laser and its dots are brighter. The cutter's are red, the grapple's light blue in both modes, where an orange
  ring shows the tether mode. Demo charges have no laser any more.
- The hip spots for the grapple and the demo charges are much smaller and further back, so reaching out to the side no
  longer draws a tool.
- Rolling with the left controller gives no thrust by itself, starts at 12 degrees of tilt and goes up to 100 degrees
  per second at 90 degrees.
- A shift starts with a level view instead of looking up at the ship.

## Fixed
- The floor of the station no longer looks tilted.
- No laser from the empty hand after using the scanner.
- The demo charges' marker goes away when they are put away.
- The hand sits on a control while working it.

## Known issues
- Leaning your real head into a wall still goes through it, and so does flying in with your head first.
- When using a loose thing such as a cable, the hand can sit a little off it.
- The control pictures do not show the flashlight and the grapple's push yet.
- Holding something heavy with the game's own grab still stops thrust, as in the flat game.
- In the cutter's Stinger mode A does nothing.
- Holding A in the grapple's tether mode while looking at a console uses the console and then recalls your tethers.
- During load screens the view can still go dark for a blink.
- There is no button for the gamepad's View button, and the left menu button stays with Virtual Desktop.
- Render resolutions above 1.0x can run out of video memory right after loading into a shift, which crashes the game.
  If that happens, lower "Render resolution" to 1.0.

## Install
Download `ShipbreakerVR-0.24.0.zip`, extract everything into the folder that contains `Shipbreaker.exe` and start the
game. See [docs/INSTALL.md](../INSTALL.md) for details and uninstalling. If you update from an earlier version, your
settings stay.
