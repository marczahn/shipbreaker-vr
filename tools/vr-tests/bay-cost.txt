# What the bay's furnaces and processors cost: frame times with their parts switched off one at a time, then with
# parts in a furnace. Start it in a shift, floating in the bay with a furnace in view (Test/Testmodus on; with
# Test/MockRuntime the frame rate is not tied to a headset, so every saving shows in the average).
# Compare the "Frame time", "Frame steps" (FixedUpdate is physics and the scripts' physics steps) and "Render steps"
# lines of the stretches in results\bay-cost\log.txt. The scene must not change in between: do not cut or move.
# The last part (bay feed) changes the shift: loose parts go into the furnace. Use a save that does not matter, or
# delete everything from "bay feed" on.
head 0 1.7 0 0 0 0
wait 2
bay survey
stats baseline
wait 20
bay flow off
stats flow off
wait 20
bay flow on
bay triggers off
stats triggers off
wait 20
bay triggers on
bay shadows off Furnace
stats furnace light shadows off
wait 20
bay lights off Furnace
stats furnace lights off
wait 20
bay lights on
bay shadows on
bay effects off Furnace
stats furnace effects off
wait 20
bay effects on
# Every light's shadows, the sun's too, as the upper limit of what shadows cost here.
bay shadows off *
stats all shadows off
wait 20
bay shadows on
stats baseline again
wait 20
bay feed 40 Trigger_Furnace
stats 40 parts in the furnace
# A furnace part is gone after 10 s (5 s heating up, 5 s dissolving).
wait 12
bay survey
stats after the furnace
wait 20
bay survey
# The last stretch's lines are logged when it ends; they need a moment before the script does.
stats
wait 1
